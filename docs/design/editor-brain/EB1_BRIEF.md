## The ten things the build lanes must not get wrong

1. **The dialogue lane is DERIVED, last, and blocking.** `sync_dialogue_lane` rebuilds `a1` from the final v1 layout as the last stage-2 step; nothing pairs lanes inside the cut/split/reorder tools; `dialogue_in_sync` is a BLOCKING EDL-only check; a brain plan never adds a v1 transition while `a1` exists; camera-mic audio on v1 angle pieces is MUTED, gain preserved.
2. **One clock in the graph: reference seconds.** Every EDP `ref` is `{src, t0, t1}` in that file's own seconds, derived by the offsets at plan time; the brain never stores a timeline second; every timeline second exists only inside a resolved arg dict for one dispatch, mapped through `agent/timemap` against the LIVE store at that step.
3. **The EDP is frozen before the dry run** and both the dry run and Apply read the same file; nothing decides at run time except the resolver's mapping; a model's answer is cached in the semantic layer with provenance and a stubbed model yields the recipes EDP byte for byte.
4. **`PLAN_DENY`, `Plan.steps ≤ 24`, `vai://plan/1`, `edl/schema.py` renderable fields and `RENDER_BEHAVIOR_VERSION` do not change in this wave.** Three narrow tools (`cut_source_ranges`, `apply_camera_plan`, `sync_dialogue_lane`) are added at stage 2; `$brain:*` is accepted only for the declared (tool, arg) pairs with a valid `plan_ref`.
5. **One run = one `store.batch()` = one `commit("prompt", …)` = one ⌘Z = one change card**, however many cuts, splits, swaps and keys fan out inside; the fan-out contract is the existing `$fit_best` list shape (`executor.py:705-719`), guarded per element.
6. **Cut edges sit on energy troughs, not token edges**: the 100 Hz `env_10ms` envelope, ±80 ms search outside every kept word's voiced span, the tie rules (later trough for an out-point, earlier for an in-point), ≥ 40 ms of air before a kept onset, 5 ms fades on every dialogue seam, `no_cut_mid_word(tol=0.02)` on AUDIO seams — camera switches over a continuous `a1` are exempt.
7. **Camera switches anticipate**: backchannel is a lookahead test (< 0.6 s or the lexicon), the switch lands at the first-word onset minus 3 frames snapped to the preceding gap; every tighten seam ≥ 0.4 s is hidden by an angle change AT the seam (or a scale step on one camera); switches never land 0.4 s late.
8. **A punch-in is a shot**: in at the clause start ≥ 0.8 s before the peak, hold through the sentence, release as a STEP at the next seam (no out-keys); `Keyframe.interp` is ONE mode per property, so never emit two easings on `scale`.
9. **Numbers count once and pauses can be kept**: `strong_number` feeds one term per formula and one hook axis, a year or a small count feeds none; a pause after emotion/laughter or before a hard answer is a `keep_pause` decision with `pause_kept:{why}` on the card, never silently removed; every join of non-adjacent scenes passes `join_ok` against the NEW predecessor.
10. **Honesty over polish**: every decision carries `{code, facts (real graph ids), text}` and the card line's why comes from the resolver's footprint; the reply names the rung per contribution; `deferred[]` lists what this wave does not do (highlight, lower thirds, intro/outro, pans, shorts, revisions, protection, the panel); the fixtures never change the existing benchmark's media or truths; no lane runs the app, a render or a download while the release gate runs.

## EB1 as built (folded in by the integrator, 2026-09-29)

The wave landed as briefed except where a measurement said otherwise. The full list is in the integrator's report; the ones a later wave must know:

1. **Offsets, one convention.** An event at reference second r is at file second r + offsets[file]. `apply_camera_plan` shifts by `offsets[angle] − offsets[src]`; `sync_dialogue_lane` lays `in_ = piece.in_ + offsets[dialogue] − offsets[piece.src]`; `dialogue_in_sync` checks the same, and accepts the tool's clamp where the dialogue file has not begun yet (the camera rolled before the recorder).
2. **Removals are on the frame grid, by the planner.** The tools round a removal OUTWARD to frames, which moved trough-placed edges up to a frame into kept sound and made the reel 0.24 s shorter than planned. The planner now rounds every removal INWARD onto the primary file's grid (a filler's removal reaches OUTWARD a frame so the whole filler goes), so the tool's rounding is a no-op and the summary's duration is the programme's, to the frame.
3. **The asked length is met in air.** Whole sentences land within ±1 s; the pauses the plan shortens then give back up to 0.45 s each (never below a 0.2 s cut) until the programme is within 0.2 s of the asked length.
4. **Overlapping removals are one, with the more specific reason**; two separated by under 0.5 s of air with no kept word are one cut. A gap between two words is cut as silence only where the audio layer is unvoiced (lane D's `unheard_voice` and the VAD bound every gap cut).
5. **A filler is its voiced island** (the VAD run ≤ 0.6 s it sits in), not its token: whisper times the next word into the tail of an "um".
6. **Camera.** Every picture change is a speaker's switch 0-0.15 s before their first SUSTAINED sound (a one-frame transient ahead of the voice is skipped; the lead is planned 0.03 s inside the clamp, the measured onset error). A seam inside a turn is hidden by a scale step, on two cameras as on one — a listener's-close cover would have to cut back mid-turn, away from every onset (reaction cutaways are rule 4, EB2). The switch-rate cap is REPORTED on the card, never enforced by leaving a speaker off camera (P2 runs at 14 turns a minute; no subset under 6 a minute keeps 90 % of the talk on the speaker's close).
7. **Punch-ins.** A line both louder (≥ 1.5 σ) and coloured (emotion ≥ 0.6) is emphasis by delivery alone (lane D's importance tops out at 0.30 on fixture TH, so the 0.5 floor by itself never fired); the peak is the loudest CONTENT word; the cadence is counted in programme time on a reel.
8. **`no_cut_mid_word` measures by the Content Graph** when the session has a current one (words repaired onto the sound, every angle's edges on the reference clock); the upload transcript is the stick only without a graph.
9. **Two cameras dropped on the timeline**: the second angle's own clip leaves the main lane first (a literal `cut_source_ranges` step), then returns through the camera plan.
10. **Files already on the timeline** may be named by the three brain tools even when they are not under the session's uploads (`facts.timeline_paths`); nothing else reads that set.
11. **The analysis gate is answered in the turn**: "read" starts the analysis job (the one function the route also calls), streams `analysis` frames, and re-plans the original sentence.
12. **Versions are named by what the edit made**: a reel is "Reel", an episode by its style ("Premium Podcast"). `Compiled.plan_id` is optional; `repeat` is in `REASON_CODES`.
13. **Provenance.** When the semantic layer holds a model's hook ranking, the EDP's `content_brain` and the hook decisions' `by` name it; a model that did not answer changes no byte.
